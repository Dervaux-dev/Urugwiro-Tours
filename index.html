Why Urugwiro Tours?

Everything you need to explore Rwanda

One platform. Two experiences. For Rwandans by district, for visitors by category.

🗺️
District-Based Discovery

Rwandans select their home district and instantly see nearby tourist sites, with local resident pricing clearly shown.

💚
Local Resident Pricing

See RWF resident rates for every site — no more confusion about which price applies to Rwandan citizens and EAC residents.

✈️
Full Visitor Directory

International tourists get a browseable directory of all 36 sites, filtered by category — parks, museums, memorials, lakes & more.

🦍
All 4 National Parks

Volcanoes, Akagera, Nyungwe, and Gishwati-Mukura — with detailed activity guides, best-time info, and permits overview.

UNESCO Memorials

All 4 UNESCO-inscribed (2023) Genocide Against the Tutsi memorials, treated with deep respect and proper historical context.
